  mu Her is an old thin disk metal-rich subgiant, basically defining
  the subgiant spectral type sequence for mid Gs -- it is one of only
  four subgiant (IV) anchor standards (Garrison94): eta Boo (G0IV), mu
  Her (G5IV), bet Aql (G8IV), and gam Cep (K1IV).  mu Her is part of
  quadruple system with three other lower mass companions.  mu Her Aa
  with Ab (M4+-1V) with recent separation ~1.8", and at 35" separation
  there is the B-C pair comprised of two mid-M dwarfs. Marcussen26
  provides a recent comprehensive review of the system with updated
  parameters.  Historically, mu Her was originally considered the dG5
  dwarf spectral standard in the 1922 Report of the IAU Committee on
  Stellar Classification.  Sandage16 mentions mu Her as one of the
  "six extreme subgiants with large trigonometric parallaxes... could
  be said to define a subgiant sequence". Star has higher metallicity
  than Hyades: [Fe/H]=+0.25+-0.01(Soubiran22/24), and isochronal ages
  are tightly clustered around ~7-8 Gyr, with the Brewer16 age of
  7.4+-0.5Gyr being a good representative estimate.  Has a 35"
  companion which itself is binary (BC: M4V,V=9.8).  V=3.42(HIP),
  B-V=0.750+-0.015(HIP), V-Ic=0.71+-0.02(HIP).
  plx=119.9248+-0.1543mas(GaiaEDR3) => Mv=3.82.  PASTEL values: <Teff>
  = 5529K(N=22), <[Fe/H]>=0.24(N=20),
  <logg>=3.975(N=22). Soubiran22/PASTEL: Teff=5513+-50K,
  logg=3.97+-0.02, [Fe/H]=+0.25+-0.01, however Soubiran24 lists
  Teff=5665+-16K, Lum=2.635+-0.020Lsun, Rad=1.686+-0.007Rsun,
  [Fe/H]=+0.25+-0.01, Mass=1.123+-0.005Msun(Soubiran24/BASTI),
  Mass=1.194+-0.115Msun(Soubiran24/STAREVOL), but more accurate
  seismic estimates: logg=4.02+-0.01(Soubiran24/seismic),
  Rad=1.692+-0.014Rsun(Soubiran24/seismic),
  Mass=1.081+-0.016Msun(Soubiran24/seismic).  Marcussen26 estimates:
  M(Aa)=1.134+-0.007Msun, M(Ab)=0.2286+-0.0006Msun,
  M(B)=0.417+-0.005Msun, M(C)=0.445+-0.005Msun, improved parallax
  plx=120.069+-0.089mas, orbit for A: Porb(Aa+Ab)=78.987+-0.288yr,
  e(Aa+Ab)=0.379+-0.001, a=20.418+-0.050au (for plx=120.069mas =>
  a=2.45157"). fbol=(122.135+-0.841)e-11W/m2(Soubiran24).  Ignoring
  the secondary, the subgiant A (using Soubiran24 luminosity, Teff)
  has Kopparapu14 optimistic hab zone limits of r_in,HZ(Aa)=1.23au,
  r_out,HZ(Aa)=2.90au.  Using Quarles20 dynamical stability limits,
  one predicts for Aa S-type stability outer limit
  a_crit(Aa)=4.76au(P_crit(Aa)=9.74yr), and for Ab
  a_crit(Ab)=1.78au(P_crit(Ab)=4.98yr). Hence the current HZ limits
  1.23-2.90au are within the dynamical limit of 4.76au.  However on
  the main sequence it would've been roughly ~F9V (logL=+0.2,
  Teff~6100K), with small HZ limits of ~0.92-2.2au, so the ~1.2-2.2au
  region should have stayed in HZ for much of the star's history.  The
  Marcussen26 mass estimate (1.134Msun) suggests that the primary was
  probably ~F8/9V during its main sequence phase. Age:
  7.0+0.6-0.4Gyr(Valenti05), 7.4+-0.5Gyr(Brewer16),
  7.88+0.24-0.23Gyr(Ramirez12), 7.88+0.40-0.36Gyr(Ramirez13),
  8.08(7.68,8.40)Gyr(Takeda07).
