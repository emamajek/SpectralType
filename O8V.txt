O8V

There is no O8V anchor standard listed by Garrison94, nor a O8V dagger standard listed by MK73. To remarkable degree, one by one all the O8V standards appear to have been adjusted to neighboring types (typically off by 0.5 subtype and/or 1 luminosity class).  At this point, none of the O8V standards from the 20th century seem to have survived as being consistently used in the 21st century.  HD 48279 was probably the best available standard (from Walborn90), but more recent works put it at O8.5V.  HD 46149 was the O8V standard in MK78 and used by Keenan and Gray, but the O-star community (Walborn, Sota+) use it consistently as
the O8.5V standard.

MK43 listed Lam Ori as the "O8" standard (from Plaskett 1922). Lam Ori arguably has the best pedigree for the "O8" temperature type, but it is now considered "O8III((f))" by Walborn and Conti.

Morgan53(1953ApJ...118..318M) appears to be the first publication that assigned luminosity class V to any O8 stars - they assigned O8V to HD 168137. 

HD 48279 and 46966 are among the best (Morgan/Walborn continuity as O8),
whereas HD 46149 is split (O8V by Morgan, O8.5V by Walborn).
