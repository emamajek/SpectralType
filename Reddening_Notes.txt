The Ammons et al. (2006) reddening values should not be used (they
employ a statistical model taking into account Galactic position and
maximum reddening through the Schlegel et al. 1998 maps). They are
better than nothing, but don't take into account real ISM features.

In the limit of low reddening A_G/A_V $\simeq$ 1.0 for
blue stars (Table 8 of Jordi et al. 2010) decreases to $\sim$0.6 for
the latest M stars (Jordi et al. 2010), hence A_G varies from roughly
0.03 to 0.02 from the hottest to coolest members).
