G9III

del Dra could probably be considered the G9III anchor, however it
is at a far nothern declination. There are no particularly good southern G9III standards.

Some G9III standards in Keenan89 were converted to G8.5III in the final Keenan99 work. 

iot Gem is possibly the best southernly accessible G9III standard, but
it is not that great of a standard: iot Gem (J0725+2747; V=3.79)
