  MS.  B-V=0.689(Tycho=>Johnson), B-V(AB)=0.693+-0.013(HIP),

  V-I=0.73+-0.02(HIP), plx=24.15mas(i.e. nearby and
  unreddened). Companion is 5 magnitudes fainter than A, hence
  unresolved colors are largely unaffected by B. PASTEL values: <Teff>
  = 5589K (N=3), <[Fe/H]> = -0.04 (N=2).  PASTEL/Soubiran22 means:
  Teff=5587+-26K, logg=3.65+-0.12, [Fe/H]=0.02+-0.05.
